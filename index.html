        Straight answers

        What does SI mean?

        SI stands for superintelligence: our name for where artificial intelligence (AI) is heading, and the word we use for the field. SI is the new AI. It's a name, not a claim that superintelligence already exists. Our hub, siagentsignal.com, is home to the SI Stylebook: how we use AI, AGI, SI and agents.

        Is this live?

        We're pre-launch, in a Founders' Preview. The free morning brief runs now (sent by our sister daily, The Agent Signal, while ours is finished), and so does the live news of the hour. Role tuning and the extra stations are rolling out. Pro, MAX and Ultra are reservations only, and we'll say plainly when each one goes live.

        Why pick a role from a list?

        A fixed list means nothing you type is sent to an AI. That keeps your brief predictable and the system safe. Missing your job? Reserve and vote for it.

        What happens to my deposit?

        It's refundable in full until you opt in: one email, no calls, no surveys. Nothing renews or converts on its own. At launch we email you a link: if you opt in, your deposit counts toward your first payment at your founding price, which you keep while you stay subscribed. If you don't opt in within 14 days, we refund it in full.

        What do you collect?

        Your email address. Optionally your first name, your role, and a mobile number only if you tick the separate text box. Our servers log IP addresses. This page keeps your choices here (role, gift, quiz, taps) on your own device only. No covert cookies, no fingerprinting, no advertising or analytics trackers. Unsubscribe in one click from any email. Privacy notice.

        Will you push tools on me?

        No ads, no affiliate links, no sponsors. Every action names two or more tools side by side, and we only sell our own products.

        Built by a real person

        SI News Daily is built by one founder with a lot of automation and one simple rule: we read every source so you don't have to. AI does the heavy reading; people set the standards.
